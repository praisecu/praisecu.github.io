<h2 id="dynamics-description">Course Description</h2>

This course covers the dynamic behavior of particle systems and rigid bodies: 2-D and 3-D kinematics and kinetics; impulse, momentum, potential and kinetic energy; and work, collisions and vibration.

**Prerequisite:** MCEN 2023.

<h2 id="dynamics-homework">Homework Policy</h2>

Homework is posted on Canvas, usually has four to five problems, and is due about one week after it is posted. Homework is submitted online, and **late homework is not accepted** after 5 PM on the due date.

Each problem is graded as:

|Grade|Credit|Meaning|
|-|:-:|-|
|Check-plus|100%|Correct answer in the required format|
|Check|85%|Correct but not in the required format, or mostly correct in the required format|
|Check-minus|50%|Largely incorrect or very hard to follow|

Not every problem in a set may be graded, but full solutions will be made available for all of them.

<h3 id="dynamics-homework-format">Homework format</h3>

To earn a check-plus, each handwritten solution should include:

- A short problem statement: the given quantities with units, and what is to be found. There is no need to copy the full problem.
- A sketch and free-body diagram, labeled with the relevant variables and geometry.
- Any assumptions used to simplify the governing equations.
- The final answer, boxed. If you use software such as MATLAB or Python, include the code and its output, written so a grader can follow it.
- Graphs with labeled axes, units, and a legend when there are two or more curves.
